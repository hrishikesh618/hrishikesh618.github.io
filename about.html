Biography

Hrishikesh Singh is a Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF, 11th cycle) and PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Water Resources Development and Management at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, working under the supervision of Prof. Mohit Prakash Mohanty. He joined the doctoral programme in July 2022 and has been a PMRF scholar since July 2023.

His doctoral research builds a socio-hydrological framework to capture flood-risk dynamics across India, pursued along two complementary pathways. At the basin scale, he has built a nationwide, depth-based hydrodynamic flood-hazard model spanning all 25 Central Water Commission river basins at 100 m resolution, coupled with a district-level socio-economic-cum-physical vulnerability index covering 680+ districts. At the city scale, he developed the Tier-based Risk Index for Cities (TRIC), a copula-based framework that ranks compound pluvial-fluvial flood risk across India's 100 Smart Cities. Together, these pathways aim to close the loop between regional flood-generating drivers and local impact.

Before joining IIT Roorkee, he completed an M.Tech in Water Resource Development and Irrigation Engineering at NIT Raipur (2020–2022, Silver Medalist) and a B.E. in Civil Engineering at Shri Shankaracharya Technical Campus, Chhattisgarh (2015–2019). He grew up and completed his schooling in Raipur, Chhattisgarh.

Supervisor: Prof. Mohit Prakash Mohanty, Department of Water Resources Development and Management, IIT Roorkee

Research interests

Large-scale flood-inundation modelling, hydrodynamic flood-hazard assessment, socio-hydrological flood-risk analysis, compound pluvial-fluvial flooding, hydroclimatic extremes, reanalysis and gridded hydro-meteorological datasets, socio-economic and physical vulnerability, remote sensing and geospatial analysis, extreme-value statistics, copula modelling, multi-criteria decision-making.

Technical expertise

  • Hydrological modelling: HEC-HMS
  • Geospatial and remote sensing: ArcGIS, QGIS, Google Earth Engine
  • Programming and analytics: Python, R, MATLAB, NetCDF processing, extreme-value analysis, copula modelling, MCDM
  • Computational infrastructure: PARAM Ganga (HPC), GPU-accelerated flood simulations
