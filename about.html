Prime Minister’s Research Fellow · PhD Research Scholar

Hrishikesh Singh

Hrishikesh Singh is a Prime Minister’s Research Fellow (PMRF, 11th cycle) and PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Water Resources Development and Management at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, working under the supervision of Prof. Mohit Prakash Mohanty. He joined the doctoral programme in July 2022 and has been a PMRF scholar since July 2023.

His doctoral research builds a socio-hydrological framework to capture flood-risk dynamics across India, pursued along two complementary pathways. At the basin scale, he has built a nationwide, depth-based hydrodynamic flood-hazard model spanning all 25 Central Water Commission river basins at 100 m resolution, coupled with a district-level socio-economic-cum-physical vulnerability index covering 680+ districts. At the city scale, he developed the Tier-based Risk Index for Cities (TRIC), a copula-based framework that ranks compound pluvial-fluvial flood risk across India’s 100 Smart Cities. Together, these pathways aim to close the loop between regional flood-generating drivers and local impact.

Research themes

The five strands the work is organised into. Full citations, impact factors and DOIs are on the Publications page.

Hydroclimatic Data Evaluation

Assessing the reliability of reanalysis, satellite and climate datasets for hydrological extremes and flood modelling across diverse river basins.

  • Published Multi-scale assessment and entropy–MCDM framework for reanalysis precipitation over Indian basins — Int. J. Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation, 2025
  • Published Can atmospheric reanalysis reproduce flood inundation at regional scales? ERA5 over the Mahanadi — Environmental Monitoring and Assessment, 2023

Large-Scale Flood Hazard & Risk Mapping

Hydrological and hydrodynamic modelling frameworks to simulate inundation, assess hazard and map flood risk across river basins and regions.

  • Published Performance of ERA5 in capturing pluvial and fluvial inundation — Water Resources Management, 2025
  • Ongoing Nationwide 100 m hydrodynamic flood-hazard model over 25 CWC basins — the core of the PMRF research
  • In preparation Multi-day design storms and differential basin flood response

Socioeconomic & Infrastructure Vulnerability

Vulnerability assessment from geospatial indicators, multi-criteria decision analysis and integrated risk frameworks.

  • Published Socioeconomic-cum-physical vulnerability to riverine floods — book chapter, Springer, 2025
  • Ongoing District-level Flood Vulnerability Index across 680+ districts, weighted by Shannon Entropy and ranked by TOPSIS

Urban & Compound Flood Risk Assessment

Pluvial–fluvial flood interactions, and integrated indices to compare and prioritise flood-prone cities.

  • Under review TRIC — a tier-based risk index ranking India’s 100 Smart Cities under compound extremes
  • Published Compound risks of floods and droughts over multi-hazard catchments — Science of the Total Environment, 2024 (co-author)

Open-Source Tools & Computational Frameworks

Reproducible workflows and accessible tools for hydroclimatic data analysis, flood modelling and geospatial processing.

  • Released CDFuse — a browser-based app for comparing any two gridded NetCDF datasets (see Data and Tools)
  • Ongoing Nationwide simulation workflow on PARAM Ganga, a National Supercomputing Mission facility at IIT Roorkee
