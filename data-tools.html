CDFuse — Compare. Validate. Visualise.

Web appOpen source

A browser-based tool for comparing two gridded NetCDF datasets. Upload a candidate dataset and a reference dataset, align them in time and space, and CDFuse scores every grid cell, maps the result, and exports it in a form you can carry into a paper or a GIS.

Metrics: Correlation, Nash–Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE), Kling–Gupta Efficiency (KGE), Percentage Bias (PBIAS), RMSE, MAE and Bias — each computed cell by cell over the matched time steps.

Handles: automatic coordinate detection, 0–360 or −180–180 longitudes, either latitude ordering, extra dimensions such as pressure level, daily/monthly/seasonal/annual aggregation, linear regridding, and optional clipping to a shapefile, GeoJSON or GeoPackage boundary.

Exports: 300 dpi PNG maps, CF-attributed NetCDF, GeoTIFF, CSV, a summary table, and a run report recording every setting used — individually or as a single ZIP.

CDFuse is deliberately dataset-neutral: it assumes nothing about any product's timestamping or accumulation convention. Time shifts and hour-of-day selection are explicit controls you set for each dataset. No installation is needed, and a built-in demonstration pair lets you try the whole workflow without uploading anything.

Launch CDFuse → Source code

First visit after a quiet spell? The app sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. If you land on a “Zzzz” screen, click Yes, get this app back up and give it about a minute — nothing is wrong, and your session starts fresh.

MIT licensed. Runs on Streamlit Community Cloud; datasets are processed in your browser session and are not stored.
